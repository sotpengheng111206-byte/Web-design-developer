• Apple





    • APPLE

    An apple is a round fruit. It can be red, green,
    or yellow. Apples are sweet or slightly sour and contain fiber and vitamin C.
    Benefits: Helps digestion and supports good health.













  • Banana

    • BANANA

    A banana is a long, curved fruit. It has yellow skin when ripe and soft,
    sweet flesh inside. Bananas contain potassium and vitamin B6.
    Benefits: Provides energy and supports muscle function.
